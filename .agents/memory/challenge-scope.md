**Why:** The user supplied this specific challenge as the basis for the project
and its judging materials. The need is secure, organized exchange among relevant
institutions with access permissions, traceable exchanges and personal-data
protection—not simply a generic court dashboard.

**How to apply:** Connect future demonstrations and explanations to obtaining
documents from other institutions, verifying official information, delayed
responses and repeated document requests. Describe intended reductions in time,
effort and administrative duplication as goals unless actual field measurements
